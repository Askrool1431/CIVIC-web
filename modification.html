LEGEND OF MODIFICATION

Akira Nakai

Akira Nakai is one of the world's most recognized automotive builders and the founder of RAUH-Welt Begriff (RWB). His handcrafted approach to building high-performance cars inspired enthusiasts around the globe to see modification as an art form rather than simply adding aftermarket parts. Although Nakai-san is famous for Porsche builds, his philosophy of creativity, precision and individuality has influenced Honda Civic builders worldwide. Every custom Civic reflects this same spirit of craftsmanship and passion.

Purple Beast

Purple Beast is a custom-built 2006 Honda Civic designed to combine everyday reliability with aggressive styling and improved performance. Essential Modification Components

Every performance build begins with carefully selected upgrades that improve power, handling, appearance and driving experience.

ECU Tuning

Optimizes fuel delivery, ignition timing and throttle response, unlocking additional engine performance.

Performance Exhaust

Improves exhaust flow, reduces back pressure and delivers a deeper sporty sound.

Alloy Wheels

Lightweight alloy wheels improve handling, braking and overall road grip.

Body Kit

Front splitters, side skirts and rear diffusers improve aerodynamics and aggressive styling.

Coilover Suspension

Adjustable suspension lowers the vehicle, improving cornering, stability and driving confidence.

Custom Wrap

Premium wraps protect the paint while creating a distinctive, personalized appearance.

Interior Upgrade

Sports seats, steering wheel, ambient lighting and premium trim create a driver-focused cabin.

Cold Air Intake

Provides cooler, denser air to the engine, improving combustion efficiency.

Performance Continues Inside The Cabin

Interior modifications complete the transformation. Racing-inspired seats, premium steering wheels, digital gauges, ambient lighting, and custom trim create an immersive driving experience while maintaining everyday comfort.
