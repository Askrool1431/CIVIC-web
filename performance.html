Factory Specification

Honda Civic 2006

The eighth-generation Honda Civic introduced a completely new design philosophy focused on fuel efficiency, driver comfort, and long-term reliability. Powered by Honda's renowned 1.8L SOHC i-VTEC engine, the Civic delivered smooth acceleration, excellent mileage, and dependable everyday performance. Its lightweight construction and well-balanced chassis made it one of the most popular compact sedans worldwide, earning a reputation that continues to this day.

MODIFIED BUILD

Purple Beast

Purple Beast transforms the factory Honda Civic into a bold street-performance machine. Every modification has been chosen to improve handling, appearance and driving experience while keeping the Civic's legendary reliability. With a lowered stance, aggressive aero kit, forged wheels, custom exhaust system and ECU tuning, this build reflects modern automotive craftsmanship and the passion of Honda enthusiasts around the world.
