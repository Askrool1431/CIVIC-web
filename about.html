More Than Just A Car

Since its debut in 1972, the Honda Civic has become one of the most successful compact sedans ever produced. Combining reliability, fuel efficiency, performance and timeless design, the Civic has earned the trust of millions of drivers around the globe.

The 8th Generation Honda Civic introduced in 2006 became one of the most iconic models due to its futuristic dashboard, sporty styling and legendary i-VTEC engine. Today, it remains one of the most modified and enthusiast-loved cars worldwide.
